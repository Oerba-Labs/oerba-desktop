Oerba Desktop 0.6.1: the Dev Panel, whenever you want it.

- **Dev Panel in the menus.** The menu bar item and the Dock menu have **Dev Panel**. It opens the panel on its own, with no app needed: on the simulator it last showed, or a running iPhone, or it starts one. The bar says to start Metro in a workspace; when you do, the notification or **Open 8081 in iOS Simulator** opens the app on the device the panel is showing. Choose **Dev Panel** again, or **✕**, to close it.
- **Choose a device before an app.** The device button in the bar works with no app open too: choose a simulator and the panel shows it.
- **Only your own ports.** A workspace's submenu no longer lists the ports its tools listen on (OpenCode's server, the editor's helpers, anything from 32768 to 60999), only your dev servers.

Oerba 0.6.0 finds this update by itself within the hour, or when your Mac wakes, and the menu bar item offers **Restart to Update to 0.6.1**.
