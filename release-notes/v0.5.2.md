Oerba Desktop 0.5.2: the iPhone panel without Pop out.

- **No Pop out.** The iPhone panel's bar now has the device's name and **✕** alone. Pop out showed the phone in Device Hub and closed the panel; the panel is where the phone is shown now, beside any of Oerba's pages, a workspace's Editor included. If you want the phone in Device Hub too, open Device Hub yourself.

This is the first update to come by itself. Oerba 0.5.1 finds it within the hour, or when your Mac wakes, and downloads it; a notification says it is ready, and the menu bar item offers **Restart to Update to 0.5.2**. Choose it and Oerba restarts into 0.5.2. Leave it, and it goes in the next time Oerba quits, or Oerba restarts itself a day later, the next time you have been away from your Mac for seven minutes or locked it, unless the iPhone panel is open.
