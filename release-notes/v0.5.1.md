Oerba Desktop 0.5.1: the panel alone, and updates that arrive.

- **Device Hub stays closed.** Open in iOS Simulator showed the phone in the panel and, behind it, in Device Hub too: serve-sim, the panel's helper, opened Device Hub each time it started. Oerba's copy of it no longer does. **Pop out** still shows the phone in Device Hub when you want it there.
- **The panel starts when port 3200 is taken.** The panel's helper was told to use port 3200 alone, so it could not start while anything else held that port, such as a workspace's port 3200 forwarded to this Mac. It now takes the first free port from 3200.

- **Restart now restarts.** It hid Oerba's window and stopped there, so an update went in only when Oerba was quit by hand. It now restarts into the new version.
- **Updates come by themselves.** Oerba looks for an update every hour and when your Mac wakes, downloads it at once, and installs it whenever Oerba next quits. When one is ready, a notification says so and the menu bar item offers **Restart to Update**. If you have not restarted a day later, Oerba restarts itself the next time you have been away from your Mac for seven minutes or locked it, unless the iPhone panel is open.

If you have an earlier version, the menu bar item offers this one: **Update to 0.5.1…**, then **Download**, then **Restart now**. That version's Restart now only hides the window: then choose **Quit Oerba** in the menu bar item, and it installs 0.5.1 and opens again. This is the last update that needs it.
