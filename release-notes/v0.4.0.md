Oerba Desktop 0.4.0: a workspace's dev server on your Mac's `localhost`.

- **Open a workspace's port on this Mac.** In the menu bar item, or by right-clicking Oerba in the Dock, choose **Workspaces**, then a workspace you have started, then **Open on localhost:<port>**. The port gets the same number here where it is free, or the next free one.
- **Web servers open in your browser** (3000, 5173, 8000, 8080). **Metro and Expo** (8081, 19000, 19006) show a notification instead, for the Simulator to load from.
- **A running forward shows in the menu** with a stop entry. It stops when you stop it, when its workspace stops, and when the app quits.
- **Web pages of other sites cannot use a forward.** It answers only on this Mac's loopback and refuses requests from other sites' pages. SECURITY.md says exactly what it refuses and why.

The app still holds no password or token, and changes nothing on a server.

If you have an earlier version, the menu bar item offers this one: **Update to 0.4.0…**, then **Download**, then **Restart now**.
