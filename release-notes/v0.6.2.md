Oerba Desktop 0.6.2: the dev panel opens at once, and from Oerba's page.

- **It opens at once.** Whichever way you open the dev panel, it appears straight away, with a spinner and what is under way (*Starting iPhone 17 Pro…*, *Opening ios-test's app…*), until the phone is on screen. If something is missing, Expo Go or Xcode, the panel says so where you are looking, rather than in a notification.
- **A Dev Panel button beside OpenCode's.** Once Oerba is updated to draw it, each workspace's room has a **Dev Panel** button beside OpenCode's. Pressed, it opens that workspace's app in the panel if its Metro is running, or the panel alone if it is not; pressed again, it closes the panel. It shows as pressed while the panel is open. Oerba draws it only in this app.

Oerba 0.6.1 finds this update by itself within the hour, or when your Mac wakes, and the menu bar item offers **Restart to Update to 0.6.2**.
