Oerba Desktop 0.3.0, the first release: Oerba in a window of its own on the Mac.

- **A setup screen** opens on the steps to get in: Tailscale installed, signed in, access to Oerba, a name. It ticks a step only once it has seen it done.
- **Your workspaces stay open** as you move between them, terminals and all.
- **In the menu bar:** open Oerba, your running workspaces, what waits on you, and a new chat.
- **Notifications, quiet on success:** a deploy of yours that failed, a workspace that failed, or something new waiting on you.
- **Two shortcuts:** ⌥Space opens Oerba's command palette, and ⌥⇧Space starts a new chat. Each is taken only if no other app holds it.
- **Updates ask first.** The app looks for a new release. Nothing downloads until you press Download, and nothing installs until you press Restart now.

**Needs** a Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later, Tailscale, and access to Oerba (the setup screen says how to ask).

**Install:** open the dmg and drag Oerba to Applications. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple. SECURITY.md says what it allows and what it refuses.
