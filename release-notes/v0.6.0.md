Oerba Desktop 0.6.0: the dev panel, and a device of your choosing.

- **Choose the device.** The panel at the right of Oerba's window (the dev panel, the iPhone panel until now) names its device in the bar as a button. It lists this Mac's simulators, the running ones first and then each iOS runtime's others, newest first, with the one shown ticked. Choose one and Oerba boots it if it is shut down, opens the same app there, and moves the panel to it. A device that is not listed is added in Xcode (Window, Devices and Simulators).
- **It remembers.** Oerba keeps the device you last opened each workspace on, so the next **Open 8081 in iOS Simulator** uses it, if this Mac still has it.
- **Metro starting is an offer.** When Metro starts listening in one of your workspaces while Oerba is open, a notification says so (**demo's app is running**, *Open it on iPhone 17 Pro*), and a click opens the app on that device, in the panel.
- **The ports your workspace is listening on.** Each workspace's submenu offers the ports it is really listening on, as On-Demand says, in place of a fixed list of usual ones; the usual ones are offered until On-Demand has answered, or if it cannot be reached. A database's port is not offered, since a forward carries HTTP and websockets alone.
- **The bar names the app.** Beside the device, the bar says which workspace and port the panel shows (**demo · Metro 8081**).

Oerba 0.5.2 finds this update by itself within the hour, or when your Mac wakes, and the menu bar item offers **Restart to Update to 0.6.0**.
