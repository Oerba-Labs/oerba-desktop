Oerba Desktop 0.5.0: the iPhone beside Oerba's page.

- **The phone in a panel.** **Open in iOS Simulator** now shows the iPhone in a panel at the right of Oerba's window, instead of opening Device Hub. Click to tap, drag to swipe, and type into the app; the buttons under the phone reload the app, press Home, reach its clipboard and turn it. **Pop out** shows the same phone in Device Hub, and **✕** closes the panel. The panel is Expo's serve-sim, carried inside the app: it answers on this Mac alone, behind a key only the panel holds, and stops when the panel closes.
- **While the panel is open**, the simulator loads a small library of serve-sim's into the apps it starts; it is taken out again when the panel closes or Oerba quits. The clipboard button installs a small helper app into the simulator the first time it copies. `SECURITY.md` says exactly what runs and what it may do.

If you have an earlier version, the menu bar item offers this one: **Update to 0.5.0…**, then **Download**, then **Restart now**.
